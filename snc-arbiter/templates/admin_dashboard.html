{{/* ── Load-factor tuning ─────────────────────────────────────────────── */}}

Load-factor balancing (bonus/malus on top of RTT routing)

Every 5 minutes the arbiter compares each node's last-hour traffic to the mean of its own type+region peers and writes a coefficient (clamped to the range below) into the signed manifest/exit list. Clients and controls multiply it into their existing RTT-based routing score — above-average load gets a malus (deprioritised), below-average gets a bonus. Regional filtering itself is untouched; this only adjusts ranking within an already-filtered pool.
