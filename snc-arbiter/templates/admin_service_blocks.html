Service Region Blocks

Destinations that a given region's exits must not dial directly, even if otherwise healthy — e.g. Telegram's voice CIDRs blocked for region RU, since RU-hosted exits can't reach them regardless of tunnel health. When a matching exit hits a matching destination, it looks for a peer exit whose own region isn't in the blocked list and relays through that instead; falls back to a direct dial if no such peer is available.
